identifAI-it doesn't use cookies, our own or third-party ones. It only uses your browser's storage, which the game needs to remember your progress and your language: local storage, plus a session copy that is erased when you close the tab.

Storage that a service you ask for needs doesn't require your consent under Spanish law (article 22.2 of Law 34/2002 on information society services).

What the game stores

identifaiit.v3
Your progress: tries, results, streaks and last choices. It lasts until you delete it.
identifaiit.idioma
Your language choice, so the game opens in it. It lasts until you delete it.
identifaiit.prueba
A quick check that storage works. It's written and removed at once, so nothing stays.

To remove all of them, clear this site's data in your browser.

If we ever add advertising, this page will change, and we will ask for your permission before any cookie is set.
