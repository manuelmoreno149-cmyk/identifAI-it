Images

You get five photos, one at a time. For each one, call it REAL (key R) or AI (key I).

  • Investigator: no time limit and no timer.
  • Normal: 3 seconds to look at each photo.
  • Bloodhound: 1 second to look at each photo.

When the time runs out, the photo is covered with a sealed evidence envelope. You can still answer, as long as you like. That's not a miss.

The timer starts only when the photo is loaded and on screen. Hiding the tab doesn't count as a miss in Images, but the clock keeps running.

You pick the mode on the briefing page, before the case starts. The game remembers the mode you chose.

Fact or Fake

You get five claims, one at a time. Decide whether each one is a FACT (key F) or a FAKE (key K).

There is no timer and no mode. Take as long as you like.

If you leave the page for more than three seconds in a row before answering, that claim counts as a miss. A shorter absence doesn't.

The text can't be selected or copied.

After each answer

The file opens and shows you the truth. In Images, you see the prompt used to create each AI image, or "Real photograph" for the real ones. In Fact or Fake, you see the truth and up to three short details.

Press Next, or Enter, to go on.

Your try

You have one try per day for each challenge. It's used when the case starts: when the countdown ends in Images, or when you press Open the case in Fact or Fake. Reading the instructions doesn't use it.

Reloading the page, changing the mode or changing the language doesn't give you another try. The try, the streak and the progress are the same in both languages.

A new case opens at midnight, in your local time.

Streak

The streak counts the days in a row on which you finished a challenge's case. A wrong answer doesn't break it. A whole day without finishing it does.

Share

Share sends a short text with your score, your streak and a link to the game. If your device can't share, it copies the text instead. The text never says which photos were AI or which claims were fake.

Keys

  • Images: R for REAL, I for AI.
  • Fact or Fake: F for FACT, K for FAKE.
  • Enter: next.
