identifAI-it is a daily game for learning to spot AI-generated images and fake facts. Every day brings one case with two challenges, and everyone gets the same ones.

In Images you see five photos and decide whether each is real or AI-generated, with three modes depending on how long you get to look. In Fact or Fake you read five claims and decide which are facts and which are fakes.

After each answer you see the case file, with the prompt behind the AI image or the truth behind the claim. That's how you train your eye.

The two challenges

  • Images. Five photos. Decide whether each one is a real photograph or AI-generated.
  • Fact or Fake. Five claims. Decide whether each one is a fact or a fake.

Both challenges share the day's theme. You can play one, the other, or both on the same day.

One try a day

You get one try per day for each challenge. Your tries, streaks and progress are the same in both languages, and they stay on your device. A new case opens at midnight, Spanish time (CET).

A personal project

identifAI-it is a personal project. There are no accounts and, for now, no ads. The game doesn't ask for personal data.
