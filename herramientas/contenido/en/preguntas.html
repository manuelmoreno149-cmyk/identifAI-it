When do new cases come out?
Every day at midnight, in your local time. Each challenge has its own five cases, the same for everyone.
Can I play again?
Each challenge gives you one try a day. Reloading the page, changing the mode or changing the language doesn't give you another try.
Why doesn't changing the language let me play again?
The try, the streak and your progress are shared by both languages. Switching to the other language is not a second chance.
What happens if the time runs out?
In Images, the photo is sealed in an evidence envelope and you can answer whenever you like. That's not a miss.
Why does leaving the page count as a miss in Fact or Fake?
Leaving the page for more than three seconds in a row, before you answer, counts as a miss for that claim. The game warns you before you start.
How does the streak work?
The streak counts the days in a row on which you finished a challenge's case. A wrong answer doesn't break it. A whole day without finishing one does. Your best streak is kept too.
Where can I see the answers from earlier days?
In the case archive. Each finished case has its own page.
Is my data saved?
Your progress and your language choice stay on your device. The game doesn't send your progress to any server. More details in the privacy policy.
Does it work offline?
No. The game needs to load the day's cases first, so you need a connection.
Who makes identifAI-it?
It's a personal project. For now it has no accounts and no ads.
How do I get in touch?
Use the contact page. There's no form: write to the email address you'll find there.
