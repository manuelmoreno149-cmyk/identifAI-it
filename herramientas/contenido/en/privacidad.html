Who is responsible

identifAI-it is a personal project. For anything about this site, the only contact is contacto@301juegodiario.es.

What the game asks for

There are no accounts, no registration and no forms. The game doesn't ask for personal data, and it doesn't send your progress to any server.

What stays on your device

Your progress and your settings are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • identifaiit.v3: your tries, results, streaks and last choices (mode and challenge).
  • identifaiit.archivo.v1: your tries and results for earlier cases played in the game.
  • identifaiit.idioma: your language choice.
  • identifaiit.prueba: a quick check that the browser's storage works. It's written and removed at once.

The game also keeps a copy of the same data in your browser's session storage, which is erased when you close the tab. If local storage isn't available, that copy is the one the game uses.

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Today: no analytics, ads or third parties

Today there is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party content, and the site doesn't load any script from Google or anyone else. Fonts, images and data are served from our own domain.

Advertising with Google AdSense (coming later)

We plan to show ads with Google AdSense, a Google service. There are no ads yet. When we turn them on:

  • Ads will only appear on the game's home screen and on the result screen, never during a game or next to the answers.
  • To load ads, your browser will connect to Google's servers, which will receive technical data such as your IP address and browser type.
  • If you allow it, Google and its partners will use cookies and other identifiers to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • Personalized ads, based on your interests, will only be shown if you consent. If you don't, Google may show non-personalized or limited ads, which use less data.
  • We will ask for your choice through Google's consent message before any advertising cookie is used, and we will update this page and the cookies page with the details.
  • You will be able to change or withdraw your consent at any time with the "Manage consent" link in the footer, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Read how Google uses this data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Hosting

Sharing

Sharing uses your device's own function, or the clipboard. The game doesn't see who you share with or what you send.

Messages

If you write to us, we use your address and your message only to reply to you.
