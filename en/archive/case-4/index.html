Case #4 · People drinking

Images

Fact or Fake

  1. FACT “The builders of Egypt's pyramids were paid part of their wages in beer.”

    Beer was a staple food in Ancient Egypt.

    • Giza workers reportedly got about 4 to 5 L (1.1 to 1.3 gal) a day.
    • It was a thick beer.

  2. FACT “Some traditional alcoholic drinks are fermented with human saliva.”

    Some traditional versions of chicha, masato and kuchikamizake were made by chewing the ingredient and spitting it out.

    • Made from corn, cassava and rice, respectively.
    • Enzymes in saliva turn starch into sugars that then ferment.

  3. FACT “Germany has more than 1,000 breweries.”

    It has around 1,500, many of them small and local.

    • Much of its beer still follows the Purity Law of 1516.

  4. FACT “Tea is the most consumed drink in the world after water.”

    It is drunk daily in almost every country, from China and India to the United Kingdom.

  5. FAKE “The oldest liquid wine preserved to this day is about 4,000 years old.”

    It is about 2,000 years old, found in a Roman tomb in Carmona (Seville, Spain) and confirmed in 2024.

    • The previous record was the Speyer wine bottle (Germany), about 1,700 years old.
