Fact or Fake

  1. FACT “The first computer "bug" in history was caused by a real insect.”

    In 1947, engineers on the Harvard Mark II found a moth trapped in a relay, causing a failure.

    • Grace Hopper taped it in her logbook: "first actual case of a bug being found."
    • That is how the term became popular.

  2. FAKE “In 1997, Microsoft developed an interface in which the keyboard was replaced by a piano.”

    There is no record of any Microsoft project to replace the keyboard with piano keys.

  3. FACT “For 8 years, the password on the computers controlling US nuclear missiles was "00000000".”

    In the Cold War, the Strategic Air Command set the launch codes of the Minuteman missiles to eight zeros.

    • It was done to bypass the locks required by the Kennedy administration.
    • That way operators would not forget the code and could launch at once.

  4. FACT “The first analog computer in history was built around 100 BC.”

    It is the Antikythera mechanism, a Greek bronze geared device found in a shipwreck in the early 20th century.

    • It predicted astronomical positions, eclipses and the date of the Olympic Games.
    • Experts date it between 150 and 100 BC.

  5. FAKE “The world's first computer weighed close to 100 metric tons (110 US tons).”

    The ENIAC, unveiled in 1946, weighed about 27 to 30 metric tons (30 to 33 US tons), far below 100.

    • It filled an entire room.
    • It is considered the first large general-purpose electronic computer.
