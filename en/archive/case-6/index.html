Case #6 · Nightclub

Images

Fact or Fake

  1. FACT “The nightclub Guinness recognized as the world's largest was in Ibiza.”

    It was Privilege, in Sant Rafel (Ibiza, Spain), with a capacity of about 10,000 people.

  2. FACT “The youngest professional DJ in the world was under 5 years old.”

    The Guinness record for youngest club DJ belongs to Britain's Archie Norbury, "DJ Archie".

    • He played at 4 years and 130 days old.

  3. FAKE “The first time a DJ performed at an Olympic Games was Beijing 2008.”

    It was Athens 2004, when Dutch DJ Tiësto played during the parade of athletes at the opening ceremony.

  4. FACT “Rod Stewart's concert on Copacabana beach is the biggest free concert in history.”

    It took place on New Year's Eve 1994 in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.

    • Guinness recognizes it as the biggest free concert.
    • Attendance was about 3.5 million people.

  5. FAKE “The disco ball was invented for the discos of the 1970s.”

    Mirror balls were already mentioned in the late 19th century, decades before disco music.
