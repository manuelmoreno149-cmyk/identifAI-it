Case #11 · Game shows

About this case

Case #11 is closed. It was the daily case for November 11, 2026 and its theme was Game shows. The detectives of that day had two challenges: five images to judge as real photographs or AI-made pictures, and five one-sentence claims to judge as facts or fakes.

  • AI-generated images: 1 of 5
  • Fake claims: 2 of 5

Below you will find every answer. Each AI-generated image comes with the prompt that produced it, and each claim with the truth behind it and the details worth remembering, so you can check what you missed.

One of the fakes: “A US network filmed the final of a game show in space, in zero gravity.” The truth: To date, no game show or reality show has filmed its final in outer space.

Want to sharpen your eye? Read our guide to spotting AI images or our guide to spotting fake news, then play today's case.

Fact or Fake

  1. Claim 1

    FACT “There is a show in Japan where the prizes are kilograms of bluefin tuna.”

    On Japanese television it is very common to reward winners with luxury food instead of money.

    • Typical prizes: bluefin tuna (maguro), wagyu beef and exclusive fruits.

  2. Claim 2

    FAKE “A US network filmed the final of a game show in space, in zero gravity.”

    To date, no game show or reality show has filmed its final in outer space.

    • Space Hero promised to send the winner to the International Space Station.
    • Costs, logistics and safety risks have prevented it.

  3. Claim 3

    FAKE “In a live game show in 2012, the defending champion suffered stress-induced aphasia and forgot how to read and write.”

    There is no record of a champion suffering stress-induced aphasia live on a TV show in 2012.

    • Suddenly losing the ability to read and write from stress is a myth.
    • Nerves and mental blocks are common on television, though.

  4. Claim 4

    FACT “The record for total winnings on a single game show is more than $4,700,000.”

    Brad Rutter accumulated about $4.8 million across different Jeopardy! tournaments.

    • In Spain, "Los Lobos" won more than 6.6 million euros on ¡Boom! (2019).

  5. Claim 5

    FACT “The biggest jackpot ever offered is 10 million dollars.”

    The biggest single prize offered on a game show is Beast Games': 10 million dollars in cash.

    • It is a reality show created by YouTuber MrBeast for Amazon.
