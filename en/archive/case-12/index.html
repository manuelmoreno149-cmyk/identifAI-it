Case #12 · Rain

Images

Fact or Fake

  1. FAKE “The most water ever recorded in a single day was 3,000 liters per square meter (118 in).”

    The official WMO record for 24 hours is 1,825 liters per square meter (1,825 mm, about 72 in).

    • It was measured at Foc-Foc, on Réunion Island, on January 7-8, 1966.
    • It happened during the passage of Tropical Cyclone Denise.

  2. FACT “The record for the longest uninterrupted rain in history is 881 consecutive days.”

    It was in Honomu Maki, on the island of Oahu, Hawaii, where it rained nonstop from October 1913 to March 1916.

  3. FACT “In Russia, residents saw nearly a thousand coins from the 16th and 17th centuries fall from the sky.”

    In July 1940, in the Russian village of Meshchery (Gorky region), a tornado lifted a buried treasure and about a thousand coins fell.

    • They were silver and gold coins from the time of Ivan the Terrible.
    • Meteorologists reached that conclusion.

  4. FACT “In 2001, in India, a red rain that looked like blood fell for more than 2 months.”

    Between July and September 2001, the Indian region of Kerala saw repeated scarlet rain.

    • At first, some physicists proposed an extraterrestrial origin.
    • It was shown to be caused by spores of a green alga (Trentepohlia).

  5. FACT “On Uranus and Neptune, solid diamonds rain down.”

    Because of the enormous pressure and heat, methane breaks down and the carbon crystallizes into diamonds that fall inward.

    • Scientists believe they sink into a sea of liquid carbon.
