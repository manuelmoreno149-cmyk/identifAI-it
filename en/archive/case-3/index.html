Case #3 · People eating

Images

Fact or Fake

  1. FACT “There is a traditional cheese that is eaten with live fly larvae inside.”

    It is casu marzu, from the Italian island of Sardinia.

    • Its name means "rotten cheese" in Sardinian.

  2. FACT “It is not legal to sell chewing gum in Singapore.”

    Sales have been banned since 1992 to keep streets clean and avoid breakdowns in public transport.

    • Since 2004 there has been an exception for therapeutic gum.
    • That gum is sold in pharmacies.

  3. FACT “One person managed to eat more than 80 hot dogs in 10 minutes.”

    Joey Chestnut ate 83 in 10 minutes in 2024, in a televised contest against Takeru Kobayashi.

  4. FACT “The biggest pizza in the world covered more than 1,200 m² (12,917 sq ft).”

    It measured 1,296.72 m² (13,958 sq ft).

    • Airrack and Pizza Hut made it in Los Angeles, USA, in January 2023.

  5. FAKE “There are more than 50,000 McDonald's restaurants in the United States.”

    The U.S. has about 13,700, which is still the most of any country for the chain.
