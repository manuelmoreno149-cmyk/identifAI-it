Case #7 · People fighting

Images

Fact or Fake

  1. FAKE “The world record for punches in one minute is more than 1,000.”

    Recorded marks are around 450 punches per minute.

    • That is about 8 punches per second.

  2. FAKE “Boxing has always had 3-minute rounds.”

    The 3-minute rounds came with the Marquess of Queensberry rules in 1867.

    • Under the first rules, a round ended when a boxer hit the floor.

  3. FACT “In an MMA fight, one fighter outweighed his opponent by more than 170 kg (375 lb).”

    It happened in Japan's Pride between Emmanuel Yarbrough, about 272 kg (600 lb), and Daiju Takase, about 95 kg (209 lb).

  4. FACT “The longest boxing match in history lasted more than 100 rounds.”

    In 1893, Andy Bowen and Jack Burke fought 110 rounds in 7 hours and 19 minutes.

    • It ended with no winner because neither could continue.

  5. FACT “Boxing was practiced more than 5,000 years ago.”

    Sumerian reliefs from Mesopotamia, from around 3000 BC, show boxing matches.
