Contact

Write to us about an error, a suggestion, a topic idea, or to ask for a correction or a removal on the site.

Email: contacto@301juegodiario.es

There's no contact form, so there's nothing to fill in on the site. Just send an email.

What to include

  • An error: the page, the challenge and the case number, if you know it.
  • A claim you think is wrong or crosses our limits: which one it is, and why you think so.
  • A correction: what is wrong, what it should say, and a source if you have one.

We can't promise a reply to every message.
