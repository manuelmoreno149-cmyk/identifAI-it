About identifAI-it

identifAI-it is a daily detective game with two challenges. Each one has five cases, and everyone gets the same five.

The two challenges

  • Images. Five photos. Decide whether each one is a real photograph or AI-generated.
  • Fact or Fake. Five claims. Decide whether each one is a fact or a fake.

Both challenges share the day's theme. You can play one, the other, or both on the same day.

One try a day

You get one try per day for each challenge. Your tries, streaks and progress are the same in both languages, and they stay on your device. A new case opens at midnight, in your local time.

A personal project

identifAI-it is a personal project. There are no accounts and, for now, no ads. The game doesn't ask for personal data.

Read the privacy policy for the details.
