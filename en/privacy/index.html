Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026.

Who is responsible

identifAI-it is a personal project. For anything about this site, the only contact is contacto@301juegodiario.es.

What the game asks for

There are no accounts, no registration and no forms. The game doesn't ask for personal data, and it doesn't send your progress to any server.

What stays on your device

Your progress and your settings are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • identifaiit.v3: your tries, results, streaks and last choices (mode and challenge).
  • identifaiit.archivo.v1: your tries and results for earlier cases played in the game.
  • identifaiit.idioma: your language choice.
  • identifaiit.prueba: a quick check that the browser's storage works. It's written and removed at once.

The game also keeps a copy of the same data in your browser's session storage, which is erased when you close the tab. If local storage isn't available, that copy is the one the game uses.

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

No analytics or tracking of our own

We don't use analytics, tracking pixels or third-party content of our own. Fonts, images and data are served from our own domain. The only third-party code the site loads is Google's AdSense script, explained below.

Google AdSense

Every page of this site loads Google's AdSense script, a Google service, so that Google can review the site and, later, show ads. There are no ads yet: no ad is shown anywhere on the site today. What this means for you:

  • When a page loads, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive technical data such as your IP address and browser type.
  • Google and its partners may use cookies and other identifiers to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Personalized ads, based on your interests, are only shown if you consent; without consent, Google may show non-personalized or limited ads, which use less data.
  • Where the law requires it, you will be asked for your choice through Google's consent message. We don't run any consent banner of our own.
  • When we turn ads on, they will only appear on the game's home screen and on the result screen, never during a game or next to the answers, and we will update this page and the cookies page with the details.
  • You can change or withdraw your consent when Google's consent message offers it, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. Ad blockers and the "do not load third-party scripts" settings of your browser also stop the script; the game works the same without it.

Read how Google uses this data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, it records visitors' IP addresses for security reasons. Read GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing

Sharing uses your device's own function, or the clipboard. The game doesn't see who you share with or what you send.

Messages

If you write to us, we use your address and your message only to reply to you.

Your rights

If we hold any data about you, you can ask to see it, correct it or delete it by writing to contacto@301juegodiario.es. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Changes

We will update this page before turning on ads or adding any other third-party service.
