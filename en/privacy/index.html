Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026.

Who is responsible

identifAI-it is a personal project. For anything about this site, the only contact is contacto@301juegodiario.es.

What the game asks for

There are no accounts, no registration and no forms. The game doesn't ask for personal data, and it doesn't send your progress to any server.

What stays on your device

Your progress and your settings are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • identifaiit.v3: your tries, results, streaks and last choices (mode and challenge).
  • identifaiit.idioma: your language choice.
  • identifaiit.prueba: a quick check that the browser's storage works. It's written and removed at once.

The game also keeps a copy of the same data in your browser's session storage, which is erased when you close the tab. If local storage isn't available, that copy is the one the game uses.

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

No analytics and no third-party content

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party content. Fonts, images and data are served from our own domain.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, it records visitors' IP addresses for security reasons. Read GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing

Sharing uses your device's own function, or the clipboard. The game doesn't see who you share with or what you send.

Messages

If you write to us, we use your address and your message only to reply to you.

Your rights

If we hold any data about you, you can ask to see it, correct it or delete it by writing to contacto@301juegodiario.es. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Changes

We will update this page before adding anything that changes how data is handled, such as a service from a third party.
