Cookies

identifAI-it itself doesn't set cookies. For the game it only uses your browser's storage, which it needs to remember your progress and your language: local storage, plus a session copy that is erased when you close the tab. The third-party code on the site is Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, and Google's AdSense script, described below.

Storage that a service you ask for needs doesn't require your consent under Spanish law (article 22.2 of Law 34/2002 on information society services).

What the game stores

identifaiit.v3
Your progress: tries, results, streaks and last choices. It lasts until you delete it.
identifaiit.archivo.v1
Your tries and results for earlier cases played in the game. It lasts until you delete it.
identifaiit.idioma
Your language choice, so the game opens in it. It lasts until you delete it.
identifaiit.prueba
A quick check that storage works. It's written and removed at once, so nothing stays.

To remove all of them, clear this site's data in your browser.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

Every page loads Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies and uses no local storage or other identifiers on your device, so it needs no consent banner. More information in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Google AdSense

Every page loads Google's AdSense script so that Google can review the site and, later, show ads. There are no ads today. Google and its partners may use cookies and other identifiers to show ads (personalized only if you consent), measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for your permission through Google's consent message, and this page will list those cookies once ads are turned on. We don't set any advertising cookie ourselves.

How to change your consent

You can change or withdraw your consent when Google's consent message offers it, clear this site's data in your browser, or block third-party scripts with your browser or an ad blocker; the game works the same without the script. More information in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
