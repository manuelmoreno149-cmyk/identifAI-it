How we pick the cases

Real photos

The real photographs come from free image banks: Unsplash and Pexels. We use them under their licenses.

AI images

The AI images are made with Gemini or ChatGPT, from prompts we write ourselves. After you answer, you can read the prompt used for each one.

The same treatment for every image

Every photo, real or AI, is converted to the same size, format and quality, and its metadata is removed. That way the file itself doesn't give anything away.

An image that has already been played never comes back on a later day.

The claims

A person writes each claim and checks it against public sources before it's published. Every claim comes with its truth and a few details, which you see once you answer.

The fakes

Some claims are false on purpose. These are the limits we set for them:

  • They never deal with health, safety, tragedies or politics.
  • They never try to damage the reputation of a person or a brand.
  • If they mention a famous person or brand, they only change harmless figures or records.
  • Every fake is explained after you answer.

Think a claim crosses one of these lines? Write to us. We will review it and take it down if needed.
